# ADR 0015: Art direction

## Status

Accepted by the project owner on 2026-11-03, amended the same day (below).

## Context

The Plan left one open decision to settle before phase 3: a propaganda-poster palette with a ration-coupon UI, or a quieter documentary grimdark. P3B.0's visual language, every mockup, and the style bible (P3C.1) depend on it.

## Options

1. **Propaganda poster.** Bright poster colors and printed-paper grain, which the horror layer then decays or redacts. Grain is wheat ochre, Zeal is vermilion stamp ink, and coupons are ration-paper green. This matches the Plan's divergence table.
2. **Documentary grimdark.** A muted, photographic look from the start. The divergence table's colors and textures would need rework.

## Choice

Option 1, propaganda poster.

## Consequences

- The board starts warm and bright, so the thesis's idealism comes first and the dread layers (P3B.12) have something to decay.
- `design/UI_LANGUAGE.md` and `art/STYLE.md` (P3C.1) build on the divergence table as written.

## Amendment, 2026-11-03: propaganda distorted by cosmic horror

The project owner refined the direction after playing the demo: the poster look alone read as propaganda but not as horror. The art keeps the visual grammar of Mao-era propaganda posters (radiant sun rays, heroic upward poses, banners, massed crowds, red and gold, flat silkscreen print) and distorts it with something demonic and Lovecraftian, far enough that no specific nation can be named.

- **Kept:** sun rays and halos, upturned heroic faces, waving banners, fields of wheat, rallies, the red, gold and cream palette, flat ink-outlined print with misregistration and wear.
- **Distorted:** the radiant sun is a vast lidless eye; its rays are pale reaching limbs; crowds share one blank face or have no faces; too many raised hands; smiles held a beat too long; things in the clouds too large to see whole.
- **Never used:** real flags, the star, the hammer and sickle, real leaders or historical figures, real slogans, Chinese characters, or any national emblem. No gore and no bodies.

This also serves the Plan's guardrail: the game critiques a system's incentives, not a people, and a distorted, unplaceable state reads as fair from mainland China, Taiwan and the West. All art made under the first direction is redone under this one.
