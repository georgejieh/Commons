# ADR 0015: Art direction

## Status

Accepted by the project owner on 2026-11-03.

## Context

The Plan left one open decision to settle before phase 3: a propaganda-poster palette with a ration-coupon UI, or a quieter documentary grimdark. P3B.0's visual language, every mockup, and the style bible (P3C.1) depend on it.

## Options

1. **Propaganda poster.** Bright poster colors and printed-paper grain, which the horror layer then decays or redacts. Grain is wheat ochre, Zeal is vermilion stamp ink, and coupons are ration-paper green. This matches the Plan's divergence table.
2. **Documentary grimdark.** A muted, photographic look from the start. The divergence table's colors and textures would need rework.

## Choice

Option 1, propaganda poster.

## Consequences

- The board starts warm and bright, so the thesis's idealism comes first and the dread layers (P3B.12) have something to decay.
- `design/UI_LANGUAGE.md` and `art/STYLE.md` (P3C.1) build on the divergence table as written.
